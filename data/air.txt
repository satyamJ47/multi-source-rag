Air is an invisible but powerful blanket of gases that wraps around our planet Earth. Even though we cannot see, smell, or taste it, air is everywhere and takes up space. It is mostly made up of two main gases: nitrogen, which makes up about seventy-eight percent of the air, and oxygen, which makes up about twenty-one percent. The remaining tiny part contains carbon dioxide, water vapor, and small amounts of other gases like argon.
Air is incredibly important because without it, life on Earth could not exist at all. Humans and animals need to breathe in the oxygen from the air every single second to keep our bodies working and healthy. Plants also rely on the air; they absorb carbon dioxide to create their own food through photosynthesis and give off fresh oxygen back into the atmosphere for us to use.
